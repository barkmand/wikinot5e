Forgiven: Mercy

Mercy Brown was a peaceful vampire that gave her heart to her brother to save him from an illness.

Mercy Spell List

Mercy grants you the following spell list which you can cast from freely as long as you have the required spell slots.

Want Not

Starting at level 2, whenever you regain health using Vampiric Drain, you can use your bonus action to give that health to another creature within 30ft instead of yourself.

Merciful Embrace

Starting at level 4, you can touch a creature (you can also target yourself) and expend an Origin Form charge to cure a disease or poison affecting the target as an action. Whenever you cure a disease or poison in this way, you and the target gain temporary hit points equal to your constitution modifier.

Waste Not

Starting at level 6, whenever you target another creature with Want Not, instead of healing them, you can choose to turn the healing into a number of temporary hit points equal to half of the necrotic damage dealt, rounded up.

Children of the Night

Starting at level 10, you gain the ability to call upon the creatures of the night. Provided that it is nighttime, you can use an action to call 1d4 swarms of bats or swarms of rats, and when outside you can choose to call 1d6 wolves. They arrive on your turn 1d4 rounds after calling them. These creatures act as your companions and obey your verbal commands. They leave when no longer needed or when your soul moves on to the afterlife. You can use this feature once per long rest.
