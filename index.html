Forgiven

The Forgiven are the saved souls of vampires who chose to leave their unholy life behind and seek repentance in the eyes of their god. After countless agonizing holy rituals, their bodies have transformed to become something closer to a mortal human, repressing both the positive and negative aspects of their vampirism. Further details can be seen HERE.

You must have a Strength and Constitution score of 13 or higher in order to multiclass in or out of this class.

Class Features

As a forgiven, you gain the following class features.

Hit Points

Hit Dice: 1d10 per forgiven level

Hit Points at 1st Level: 10 + your Constitution modifier

Hit Points at higher Levels: 10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier per forgiven level after 1st

Proficiencies

Armor: Light armor, shields

Weapons: Simple weapons, Heavy martial weapons, rapiers

Tools: none

Saving Throws: Strength, Constitution

Skills: Choose two from Athletics, Deception, History, Medicine, Intimidation, Perception, Religion, Stealth

Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

  • (a) any martial weapon with the Heavy property or (b) a rapier
  • (a) leather armor or (b) any simple weapon
  • A wooden stake, a shield, a holy symbol, and a priest's pack

Unarmored Defense

Beginning at 1st level, while you are wearing no armor, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

Divine Trials

Beginning at 1st level, your god demands tests of faith from you to keep you on the right path. Choose a god from the gods table. Following your chosen god's ways keeps you in their favor, while straying away from them earns you their wrath.

You start with 3 Faith points, and you lose Faith points every time you commit a sin, depending on the graveness of that sin (a sin is defined by the morals of your chosen god). You gain Faith points by being blessed by a priest of your faith, or you can regain one Faith point by spending 30 minutes in prayer before going to bed during a long rest.

At the end of each long rest, look at your Faith. If you have less than 3 Faith points, your faith falters, and your god takes notice. Your DM can now choose to make you fail a successful ability check once until your next long rest.
If you have 3 or more Faith points, your faith holds firm, and your devotion is rewarded. You can now choose to add 1d6 to one of your failed ability checks once before your next long rest.

Origin Form

Beginning at 1st level, you can draw on your original powers as a vampire and take on an undead appearance by saying a short prayer. While you are holding your holy symbol, you can expend an Origin Form charge (as shown on the Origin Form Charges table) to reunite with your undead origins for one minute as a bonus action. While in this form, you can use Vampiric Drain as an action and Draining Bite as an action. You can go out of the form early as a free action.

Vampiric Drain

Vampiric Drain is a melee attack that has a reach of 5ft and deals necrotic damage based on your level (as shown on the Vampiric Drain Damage table), and you regain health equal to half the necrotic damage dealt rounded up. It uses your Spell Attack Bonus to hit.

Draining Bite

Bite into the neck of a grappled, restrained, or incapacitated target within 5 ft and suck out their blood. If the target is aware of the attacker, they can make a constitution saving throw to reduce the effects. On a failed save, they take necrotic damage equal to your Vampiric Drain damage, and you gain temporary hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt (these temporary hit points do not automatically disappear after a long rest), and the target gains the Bloodless condition if they don't already have it. On a successful save, the damage is halved and the target does not gain the Bloodless condition.
When you drain the blood of a creature in this way, your Faith points drop to 0 if you have any Faith points.

Sanguine Hunger

Beginning at 1st level, if you go for 7 days without draining the blood of a creature, your intelligence becomes 5 and you enter a blood frenzy, returning to your origin form until sated and attack the nearest target you can see every turn until you use Draining Bite on a creature. If no sensible targets are available, the DM gains control over your character with the primary goal of draining another creature's blood.

Undead Origin

Starting at 2nd level, you start to take on the traits of your Undead Origin. Your origin grants you features at 2nd level, and again at 4th, 6th and 10th level.

Spellcasting

Starting at 2nd level, you can call upon your god's magic and regain some of the power you have lost during your transformation. You know Vampiric Wrath as a cantrip. (Necromancy. Somatic components. Action. 1 hour duration. Make a melee spell attack against a creature within 5ft. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 (increases by 1d8 at level 5, 11, and 19) necrotic damage and until the spell ends, the target has a visible necrotic rash on the affected part of their body). You also have spell slots as noted on the Spell Slots and Spell Slot Level tables that you can use to cast spells from your Undead Origin's spell list. Constitution is your spellcasting modifier.

Circle of Mortality

At 3rd level, you gain the ability to manipulate the line between life and death. When you would normally roll one or more dice to restore hit points with a spell to a creature at 0 hit points, you instead use the highest number possible for each die.

In addition, you learn the Spare the Dying cantrip. For you, it has a range of 30 feet, and you can cast it as a bonus action.

Ability Score Improvement

At 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can take the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat from the Feats list.

Sanguine Embrace

Starting at 5th level your sanguine hunger empowers you further. Whenever you hit a creature with your Vampiric Drain, you can expend one Origin Form charge to attempt to grapple the target with advantage as a bonus action.

Vengeful Curse

Starting at 7th level, you can put a powerful curse on an enemy at the cost of your own life.

When you die, you turn into a cloud of mist that cannot take any actions. Immediately after taking this form, you can haunt another creature within 100ft of you, drawing close to their soul and putting a fog around their judgement. If you do not choose a target, you can still choose to do so on each of your turns for 1 minute until your soul moves on. For 24 hours or until the creature dies, it has -1 to all attack rolls, ability checks, saving throws, and its AC goes down by 1. Its speed is also reduced by 5ft. Once the creature dies, your soul moves on to the afterlife, but if the creature does not die within 24 hours, your soul is trapped in limbo until the creature dies.

Vampiric Drain - Improved Critical

Starting at 8th level, you gain an understanding of the weak points of your enemies. Your Vampiric Drain now crits on a 19 or 20 roll on the d20.

Shapeshift

Starting at 9th level, you regain the power to shapeshift into a bat as an action while you are in your origin form. The bat has 1d4 + your Constitution modifier Hitpoints, a flying speed of 30ft, a movement speed of 5ft, and you can still cast your Forgiven spells while in this form. You cannot otherwise use your normal actions in this form other than a 1d4 piercing damage bite. You can transform back in a puff of smoke as an action. If you get incapacitated during your transformation, you are forcibly transformed back and knocked prone.

Child of Faith

Starting at 10th level, you gain resistance to radiant damage, you regain two Faith points instead of one when praying during a long rest, and you can expend 5 faith points to reset the timer on your Sanguine Hunger.
