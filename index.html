Forgiven

The Forgiven are the saved souls of vampires who chose to leave their unholy life behind and seek repentance in the eyes of their god. After countless agonizing holy rituals, their bodies have transformed to become something closer to a mortal human, repressing both the positive and negative aspects of their vampirism. Further details can be seen HERE.

You must have a Strength and Constitution score of 13 or higher in order to multiclass in or out of this class.
