Forgiven Miscellanious Features

Choose one or more from this list of traits when you select this class:

1. You have a mild sensitivity to sunlight and running water.

2. Holy water gives you an allergic reaction and being on holy grounds gives you a headache.

3. Silver irritates your skin and you have a mild allergy to garlic.

4. Your skin is unnaturally cold to the touch.

5. You blinked. Once. Last week.

6. When you are asleep, you don't appear to breathe.

7. Your heartbeat seems so faint that it cannot be detected by standard means.
